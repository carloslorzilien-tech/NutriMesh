# Demo script — 2 minutes

## 0:00 — Home

Open the live URL. One sentence, one button. Say: hotels in Santo Domingo throw away food at the end of every service because coordinating pickup by phone leaves no record. NutriMesh is the record.

## 0:25 — Hotel logs a batch

Sign in as hotel.europa@nutrimesh.dev / demo1234. Select "Sealed store-room goods". Enter: sealed chicken breast, 18 kg, 3 °C. Try typing 5 °C — the form blocks it. Set it back to 3 and submit. The batch appears immediately.

## 1:00 — Bank confirms

Sign in as coordinador@bard.org.do / demo1234. The stats bar shows 1 batch to pick up. Click the row. Assign a driver name. Confirm. Status changes to CONFIRMED.

## 1:30 — Driver picks up

Open /c/tok-b1 in a new tab — no account needed. Press "I have arrived". Enter 3 °C and confirm weight. Press "Mark as picked up". Back in the bank tab: the batch moves to In transit.

## 1:50 — Bank closes the batch

Click the in-transit batch. Enter actual received weight (e.g. 17.5 kg). Press Accept. The stats bar updates. The closed tab shows meals and CO₂e — labeled as assumptions.

## 2:00 — End

The whole pickup from log to receipt, with a paper trail, in under two minutes. No phone call. No spreadsheet.
